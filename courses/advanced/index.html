Questions

FAQ

Do I need to take the free Level 1 course first?

No. Level 1 is recommended, not required. You can start any Advanced course directly.

How do I get my exam access code?

Payhip sends one unique code per purchase, on the download page and in your receipt email. Each code works for one course and is tied to the first email that claims it.

How does the exam work?

The exam has 40 multiple-choice questions, and you pass with 28 or more (70%). There is no time limit, and you can retake it as often as you like with the same code.

What do I get after I pass?

A Level 2 certificate with a unique number, sent to your email. You can download it as a PDF, add it to LinkedIn, and anyone can verify it on medjay.pro.

How much is a course?

$29 per course at the launch price. When a course's first 50 exam codes run out, its price rises to $39.

Can I buy for my team?

Yes — each learner needs their own code. For team pricing, book a discovery call.
