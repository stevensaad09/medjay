MEDJAY Enterprise Brokerage Program: USA Edition

A complete, trainer-led sales enablement program for US residential brokerages, delivered internally by your own Sales Manager, Senior Broker or L&D lead.

Request a proposal View All Programs →

Who It's For

  • US residential brokerages that train their own agents in-house.
  • Sales Managers who want to shorten the gap between hiring and productive client-facing work.
  • Delivered by your own team. No external preparation materials needed.
  • Individual agents: see the Self-Study Edition.

What Your Brokers Will Be Able to Do

  • Open a conversation so the other person chooses to continue it, in phone, text, email, meetings and showings.
  • Get clients to tell them what really matters, in their own words, without interrogating them.
  • Run every cold call the same respectful, compliant sequence.
  • Explain the numbers of a property clearly enough that the client can decide for themselves.
  • Find out what an objection is really about before answering it.
  • Help clients reach clear decisions they own, without pressure and without letting deals drift.

What's in the Package (150 files)

  • 10-Day Onboarding Program: Trainer Guide, Master Deck and Participant Workbook for each day, plus a Final Assessment.
  • 12-Week Core Curriculum: twelve 90-minute sessions, each with Trainer Guide, Master Deck, Participant Workbook, Assessment and Answer Key.
  • Sales Playbook (93 pages): one page per week, a 100-objection matrix, scripts, listing presentation, buyer consultation, negotiation and a one-page field card.

  • Delivery Operations Guide, Glossary and Certificate Template.
  • Editable Word, PowerPoint and Excel files, plus PDFs of all participant materials.

How Each Session Runs

90 minutes in four phases: Knowledge, Attitude, Skills and Up-Skilling, where participants perform the skill live and are scored. Recommended: one session per week, with manager follow-up within 48 hours.

Delivery Sequence

  • New hires: 30-Day Ramp Plan (Onboarding runs inside it), then Weeks 01–12, then Certification Day.
  • Experienced brokers: start at Week 01. Onboarding is optional.

Assessment and Certificate

  • Weekly assessments: 60 points (Know 10, Decide 20, Execute 30). Pass 48/60 with no critical failure.
  • Week 12: a 30-point written test plus the Graduation Rubric on Certification Day.
  • The MEDJAY Enterprise Advisor certificate is issued by your brokerage, powered by MEDJAY. Valid 12 months, renewed by review. Not an accredited qualification, CE credit or real estate license.

License

  • Perpetual, internal-use license by number of learners. Run any number of cohorts within that number.

  • You may add your own verified market data, examples and policies.

FAQ

Who delivers the training?

Your own Sales Manager, Senior Broker or L&D facilitator. Each Trainer Guide includes a slide-by-slide run sheet, role-play guidance and coaching language.

Do we need to run the onboarding?

Only for new hires. Experienced brokers start at Week 01.

What does "learners" mean in the license?

The total number of people trained over the life of the license, across any number of cohorts.

Can we measure the impact?

Yes, with the optional Pilot Measurement Kit: baseline, weekly KPIs and Pilot Reviews at Weeks 4, 8 and 13. MEDJAY does not guarantee sales outcomes.

Is it compliant with US rules?

The program teaches inside TCPA, the FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule, CAN-SPAM, Fair Housing and the 2024 NAR practice changes. Your brokerage stays responsible for its own compliance and for verifying market figures before use.
