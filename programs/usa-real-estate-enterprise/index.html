The Difference

Built for a Brokerage, Not a Single Desk

The Individual Track is self-study for one agent. This is a delivery system for a team — a Sales Manager stands in front of the room, runs the exercises, scores the live role-plays, and coaches the follow-through. Same behavioral science underneath (dual-process theory, tactical empathy, the Challenger Sale) — different delivery model, built for consistency across every desk in the brokerage.

The Program

How It's Delivered

A Sales Manager Runs This. Not a Login Page.

Every week is a 90-minute live session, delivered in order — the vocabulary and muscle memory from earlier weeks compound into later ones. Your Sales Manager reads the Trainer Guide before the room, not the other way around; the substance — the psychology behind each technique, and where the evidence boundary sits — lives in the guide, not the slide.

Delivery cadence: one module per week, 90 minutes of core training and live practice, with a Field Application assignment due before the next session. Managers get a Manager Observation Scorecard and a Coaching Language Bank for every week's debrief.

The Math

What This Is Built to Move

Every module in this program is built around the two moments that kill most brokerage deals: the first ten seconds of contact, and the close. A brokerage that fixes those two moments consistently, across every desk, doesn't get a one-off win from one agent's good day — it gets a repeatable shift across the whole team's pipeline.

Based on the deal volume a mid-sized brokerage's closed-deal rate typically improves by after a structured objection-handling and closing overhaul, we estimate a program like this returns somewhere in the 80–300% range on program cost within the following 12 months — the wide range reflects how much depends on your team's current baseline, deal size, and how consistently the program is actually run.

*This is a planning estimate, not a guarantee — actual results depend on your brokerage's baseline conversion rate, average deal size, team size, and delivery consistency. We'll work through your specific numbers on the discovery call.

The question for a brokerage isn't whether a training budget is worth it. It's how much of your pipeline is currently leaking through the same two conversations, desk after desk, that this program is built to fix.
