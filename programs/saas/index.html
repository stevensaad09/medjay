The Curriculum

Three Tracks. One Clear Path
From First Call to the Boardroom.

Each track is a complete, standalone certification — with its own facilitator guide, assessment, and workbook. Take them in sequence, or start wherever your team's skill gap actually is.

Track 01 — Foundations

SaaS Sales Mastery: Beginner

4 modules 61 slides 8 hours

For SDRs, junior AEs, and anyone new to selling software as a recurring commercial relationship instead of a one-time transaction. Builds the financial and behavioral literacy every later track assumes.

Module B-01

The SaaS Business Model

ARR, MRR, churn, LTV:CAC, and NRR — and why discounting a bad-fit deal is worse than losing it. The MRR Waterfall and Feature-Benefit-Outcome translation.

Module B-02

Decoding the SaaS Buyer

Mapping the buying committee, System 1 vs. System 2 decision psychology, and reframing pitches with loss aversion instead of feature lists.

Module B-03

Precision Prospecting

The 3-Layer ICP, behavioral trigger events, and a 3-touch outbound sequence built to replace spray-and-pray outreach.

Module B-04

Advanced Enterprise Discovery

The pain hierarchy (technical → business → personal), quantifying Cost of Inaction, and a first pass at MEDDPICC and TEDW questioning.

Track 02 — Execution

Advanced SaaS Sales: Intermediate

5 modules 100 slides 11 hours

For reps who can run a discovery call but still lose deals to unmapped stakeholders and soft forecasts. This is where MEDDICC becomes muscle memory.

Module I-01

Advanced Discovery Architecture

A 3-stage discovery sequence — Surface, Deep, Strategic — plus mirroring, labeling, and strategic silence as execution techniques.

Module I-02

Advanced SPIN Selling Mastery

Full SPIN methodology integrated with MEDDPICC, built around Situation, Problem, Implication, and Need-Payoff questioning under real deal pressure.

Module I-03

MEDDICC: Full Qualification Depth

Scoring deal risk across all 7 MEDDICC dimensions, and a Champion Strength framework that separates a real Champion from a friendly Coach.

Module I-04

Stakeholder Mapping & Multi-Threading

Identifying Advocates, Skeptics, and Detractors, and the "Phantom Blocker" pattern that kills late-stage deals no one saw coming.

Module I-05

Pipeline Hygiene & Forecast Discipline

The Golden Rule of Forecasting, strict Commit/Best Case/Pipeline categories, and a live pipeline scrub methodology for managers.

Track 03 — Enterprise

Enterprise Revenue Mastery: Advanced

5 modules 119 slides 19 hours

For senior AEs, Key Account Managers, and revenue leaders operating at the $500k–$15M deal size, where the buyer is a boardroom, not a person.

Module A-01

Mastering Complex Negotiation

BATNA diagnosis, diminishing concession architecture, and navigating procurement without losing access to the Economic Buyer.

Module A-02

C-Suite Selling & Executive Presence

An archetype map for the CEO, CFO, CTO, CPO, and CISO, and the 5-part structure behind every board-ready executive briefing.

Module A-03

Executive Presence & Political Navigation

The companion module to A-02: reading the room, the "Ghost Executive" who can kill a deal without ever joining a call, and tactical empathy in practice.

Module A-04

Strategic Key Account Management

The five dimensions of account health, white space mapping, and rebuilding a KAM's role around Net Revenue Retention, not renewal paperwork.

Module A-05

Enterprise Revenue Governance & Expansion

The capstone module: NRR as an economic engine, the Deal Squad model, and running expansion like a general contractor runs a build.
