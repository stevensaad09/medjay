USA Real Estate

MEDJAY Elite Training: USA Self-Study Edition

For independent US residential real estate agents with some field experience.

A 12-week self-study program in sales psychology, from first-contact standards and discovery to objection handling and the Commitment Close. Every technique comes with its source, its limits and the US rules that apply to it. Includes three monthly assessments, AI role-play practice and a 93-page sales playbook.

Built Around
  • Cold Call Psychology (with TCPA and Do Not Call rules)
  • Frame Control
  • Objection Handling Matrix
  • The Commitment Close

UAE Real Estate

MEDJAY Elite Training: UAE Self-Study Edition with Beginner Kit

For people new to UAE real estate and brokers in their first year.

Selling to Contractors
