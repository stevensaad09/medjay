How the Levels Connect

Level 1 gives reps the tools: the Counter Flow, the Question Ladder, A.I.M. offers and A.R.T. for objections. Level 2 teaches team leads to coach those tools with SBI feedback and to communicate upward. Level 3 teaches managers to hire for those behaviors, set standards around them and run the branch as a business.

A Typical Day (about 3 hours)

  • Review of the last on-the-job challenge
  • The rookie mistake the day corrects
  • The psychology behind it, in plain language
  • The day's tool, practiced in pairs or small groups
  • A worked scenario, then triad role-plays with an observer
  • Key takeaways, a 5-question practice quiz and the next on-the-job challenge

Every day is built on KASU objectives: Knowledge, Attitude, Skill and an Upskill challenge completed on the job.

Assessment and Certificate

  • Final Exam per level: 30 multiple-choice questions in 45 minutes. Pass: 24 correct (80%).
  • Day 10 role-play: scored on 6 behaviors. Pass: Meets or better on at least 5, including the level's key behavior (Level 1: Honesty; Level 2: Fairness and Integrity; Level 3: Standards and Integrity).
  • One retake of the failed part only, at least a week later, after coaching.
  • Participants who pass both parts receive a certificate of completion issued by your company, powered by MEDJAY. Valid 12 months, renewed by review. Not an accredited qualification, CE credit or license.

FAQ

Who delivers the training?

Your own sales leaders, branch managers or L&D team. Each Trainer Guide has a timed agenda, role-play cards and facilitation notes.

Can we buy only one level?

Yes. Each level is licensed on its own. Level 3 works as a standalone entry point for branch and area managers.

How do we schedule it?

Ten consecutive working days per level, or two days a week over five weeks, with time between days for the on-the-job challenge.

Does it cover pricing and hiring law?

Level 3 includes Legal Boundary sections on pricing, competitors and hiring. They are training summaries, not legal advice. Your legal or HR team reviews them before delivery.

Does it guarantee results?

No. Results depend on how the program is delivered and applied.
