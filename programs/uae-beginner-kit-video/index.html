Who It’s For

Who It’s For

  • People new to UAE real estate and brokers in their first year — start here, then take the 12-week video course.
  • People considering a broker career who want the foundations first — how the market works and the rules — before they start selling.
  • Not for: teams or brokerages training several brokers. They need the Enterprise Brokerage Program.
What You Will Be Able to Do

What You Will Be Able to Do

  • Carry yourself like a professional: listen properly, stay composed under pressure, and handle the word “no” without losing the conversation.
  • Explain how the UAE property market works: off-plan vs secondary, the portals, and the deal stages from viewing to transfer.
  • Know the licensing route: how a broker gets licensed, and what professional conduct the regulators expect.
  • Read the contracts you will meet, and know what each one does before you sign it or explain it to a client.
  • Advertise truthfully: the permits you need and the marketing claims you must not make.
  • Explain escrow and off-plan rules honestly, so buyers understand where their money goes and what protects it.

FAQ

No. It prepares you for the field and complements the regulator’s licensing training. It does not replace that training and it is not a broker licence. Do not deal with clients until you are licensed in the emirate where you work.

No. The video lessons teach the method step by step, and the course is built for self-study.
