A 12-week sales psychology program for licensed US residential real estate agents. Calm, evidence-based selling that respects the client's choice.

Who It's For

  • Licensed, independent US residential agents with some field experience.
  • Agents who want a repeatable way to open, discover, handle objections and close, without pressure tactics.
  • Not for: teams or brokerages training several agents. They need the Enterprise Brokerage Program.

What You Will Be Able to Do

  • Open a conversation so the other person chooses to continue it, in phone, text, email, meetings and showings.
  • Get clients to tell you what really matters, in their own words, without interrogating them.
  • Run every cold call the same respectful, compliant sequence, whatever your mood.
  • Explain the numbers of a property clearly enough that the client can decide for themselves.
  • Find out what an objection is really about before you answer it.
  • Help clients reach clear decisions they own, without pressure and without letting deals drift.

The 12 Weeks

Month 1: Foundations

  • 01 The Psychology of First Contact: how to open a conversation the other person chooses to continue.
  • 02 Trust Engineering & Discovery: mirroring, silence and priming that turn trust into usable information.

  • 04 The Cold Call Blueprint: one repeatable, legal outbound sequence.
  • Month 1 Final Assessment

Month 2: Applied Psychology

  • 05 Decoding High-Ticket Buyer Avatars: seeing the property through each buyer's decision lens.
  • 06 Frame Control & Authority Engineering: bringing a drifting conversation back to the real decision.
  • 07 The Challenger Reframe: teaching clients what they do not yet know, with evidence.
  • 08 Financial Persuasion & ROI Mastery: presenting the numbers honestly and clearly.
  • Month 2 Final Assessment

Month 3: Mastery

  • 09 The Objection Handling Matrix: diagnosing the objection before answering it.
  • 10 Client Retention & Trust Architecture: making your unique value visible.
  • 11 Tactical Closing & Deal Acceleration: decisions the client owns, deals that do not drift.
  • 12 The Capstone Simulation
  • Month 3 Final Assessment

How Each Week Works (about 3.5 to 4.5 hours)

  • Study Guide (24–30 pages): the science with sources and limits, the week's framework, five sale stages across five channels, five US client situations, three annotated conversations, a case study and US rules.
  • Workbook & AI Practice (8–13 pages): exercises on your own pipeline, AI role-play prompts with scoring, a field assignment and model answers.
  • Knowledge Check: five questions, pass 4 of 5.

Assessments and Completion

  • Three Monthly Final Assessments: 20 scenario questions plus a written response. Pass: 16 of 20, both critical questions correct, and 12 of 15 on the written part.
  • Week 12 Capstone Simulation, scored on the Graduation Rubric: at least 4 of 5 on every pillar, no critical failure.
  • Completion is recorded in your own Completion Record. It reflects your own practice and self-assessment. It is not a license, continuing-education credit or an accredited qualification.

FAQ

Do I need a trainer?

No. Each Study Guide replaces the live demonstrations a trainer would give, and the Workbook gives you model answers to check yourself.

How long does it take?

12 weeks at about 3.5 to 4.5 hours a week. If a week takes longer, take longer.

Do I get a certificate?

Not with this edition. You record your own completion in the Completion Record in the Week 12 Workbook.

Which AI tool do I need?

Any assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Never paste real client details into it.

Can my team use one copy?

No. The license covers one person. Teams and brokerages need the Enterprise Brokerage Program.

Does it guarantee results?

No. It teaches skills and judgment. All examples and case studies are illustrative composites, not client results.
