Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who we are

MEDJAY is operated by Steven Saad in Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. We decide how the personal data described here is used. Questions or requests: Steven@medjay.pro.

What we collect and why

  • Free course downloads. When you open a course download page or download a file, we record the course, the file, your country and the date. We do not store your IP address, name or email with these records. Your country comes from our hosting provider, and we keep only the two-letter code. We use these counts to see which courses are useful.
  • "Stay in touch" form (optional). Your email, role, team size, the course you were viewing, your country and the date. We use them to send you emails about the course and our training. If you are a manager or business owner with a team, Steven gets an email alert so he can contact you personally.
  • Newsletter. Your email address, to send you the newsletter.
  • "Customize Your Course" requests. Your name, email, industry, team size and what you tell us you need. This is sent to Steven by email so he can reply. It is not stored in our website database.
  • Course exams. When you submit a free course exam, we store the course, the answers you chose, your score, whether you passed and the date. No name or email is attached unless you go on to claim a certificate. We use this to grade the exam and issue certificates.
  • Course certificates. If you pass and claim a certificate, we collect your full name and email address, and link them to your exam answers and score. We use your name to print the certificate, your email to send you the certificate link and to make sure each person gets one certificate per course, and the record so anyone can check the certificate is genuine. Your name, the course, the issue date and the certificate number are shown publicly on the certificate page and the verification page (medjay.pro/verify/<number>) to anyone who has the number. Your email address and score are never shown there. If you tick "Send me MEDJAY updates and new courses", we also add your email to our course mailing list.
  • Article comments. Your name, rating, comment and the date. Comments are shown publicly on the article.
  • Spam checks. The comment form and the course exams use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots. It checks your browser, and we send your IP address to Cloudflare for that check only. We do not store it.
  • Running the website. Our hosting provider processes technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver pages and protect the site.

Legal basis

  • Consent: the "Stay in touch" form, the newsletter, the "MEDJAY updates" box on the certificate form, and comments. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: anonymous download and page-open counts (to improve the courses), spam and security checks, delivering the website, and following up on a team enquiry you sent us.
  • Your request: replying to a "Customize Your Course" request, and grading your exam and issuing, emailing and verifying the certificate you claim.

Who processes your data for us

  • Cloudflare: hosting, our database (including exam and certificate records), security and spam checks.
  • Brevo: our email lists, the emails sent to Steven, and the email that sends you your certificate link.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. Paid courses and books are bought on Payhip's website, under Payhip's own privacy policy.

International transfers

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the European Union and the United States. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on the safeguards the providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Download and page-open counts: kept for statistics. They do not identify you.
  • "Stay in touch" and newsletter contacts: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, or 24 months after our last contact with you, whichever comes first.
  • "Customize Your Course" requests: in Steven's email for up to 24 months.
  • Comments: until we remove them or you ask us to.
  • Certificates: your name, email, exam answers and score are kept for 10 years from the issue date, so the certificate stays verifiable, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Exam attempts without a certificate: the answers, score and date are kept for statistics. They are not linked to your name or email.

Cookies

We do not use advertising cookies, and our pages do not set cookies of their own. Our hosting provider may use cookieless analytics to count visits, and may set a strictly necessary security cookie to protect the site from abuse. The admin area uses a login cookie for site administrators only.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or send it to another service. You can withdraw consent at any time. Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the UAE Data Office, the authority in your EU country, or the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

How to request deletion or access

Email Steven@medjay.pro and tell us what you would like. We may ask you to confirm that the email address is yours. We reply within one month. Download counts cannot be linked to you, so there is nothing personal there to find or delete.

Children

This website and our courses are not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Course certificates

Certificates are issued for the free Level 1 courses when you pass the exam. We collect your full name, your email address and your exam answers and score. We keep this record for 10 years from the issue date so that employers and others can check the certificate is genuine. Anyone with the certificate number can see your name, the course, the issue date and whether the certificate is valid on the verification page; your email address is never shown. To ask us to delete your certificate data, email Steven@medjay.pro from the address you used. After deletion, the certificate can no longer be verified. Cloudflare stores the record and Brevo sends the certificate email for us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For important changes, we will also tell subscribers by email.
